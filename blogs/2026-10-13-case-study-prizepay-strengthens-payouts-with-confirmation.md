PrizePay is a platform that helps businesses distribute cash rewards, providing consumers with a fast and simple way to receive their rewards. As payout volumes increased, PrizePay wanted to reduce the risk of delays or misdirected payouts caused by incorrect account details, while maintaining a simple customer experience. PrizePay partnered with Azupay to integrate Confirmation of Payee into its payout process, giving PrizePay greater confidence payments are going to their intended recipients […]
